After completing the Core, you can choose one or more of the following three sections depending on your goals:

  • Project Management Methodologies (Comparative): Choose this if you want to understand different ways projects can be managed (traditional, agile, hybrid, etc.) and when to use each.
  • Governance, Programs & Portfolios: Choose this if you are interested in how multiple projects are coordinated at the organizational level, or if you aim for roles in program management, portfolio management, or PMO leadership.
  • Advanced & Contextual Topics (Electives): Choose this if you want exposure to modern trends (sustainability, data-driven project management) or real-world industry examples.

# Great Readings

A curated collection of field-defining monographs, seminal empirical papers, and landmark treatises that shaped the intellectual foundations of Project Management.

> _The greatest challenge of project management is not calculating the critical path, but understanding how human systems, cognitive limitations, and organizational structures interact under conditions of uncertainty._

These texts move beyond standardized survey manuals to explore how master researchers and practitioners conceptualize temporary organizations, navigate cognitive biases, model dynamic rework feedback loops, and manage complex systems.

Other curricula

This curriculum is one of a few efforts to inform learners how they can pursue [Field] independently. Below are a few other such efforts.

  • Google Project Management Professional Certificate (Coursera) – A beginner-friendly, job-ready program with hands-on projects and AI integration.
  • PMI Free Online Courses and Resources – Self-paced modules from the Project Management Institute, including Kickoff basics and certification prep.
  • Alison Free Project Management Diploma – Comprehensive, no-cost diploma covering full project lifecycles with real-world applications.
  • OpenLearn Project Management Modules (The Open University) – Free, flexible units on key topics like risk and stakeholder management.
  • SSGI Project Management Certification – Practical, self-paced alternative to PMP with case studies and lifetime access.

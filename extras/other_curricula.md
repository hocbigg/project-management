# Other curricula

This curriculum is one of a few efforts to inform learners how they can pursue [Field] independently. Below are a few other such efforts.

- [Google Project Management Professional Certificate (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-project-management) – A beginner-friendly, job-ready program with hands-on projects and AI integration.
- [PMI Free Online Courses and Resources](https://www.pmi.org/learning/free-online-courses) – Self-paced modules from the Project Management Institute, including Kickoff basics and certification prep.
- [Alison Free Project Management Diploma](https://alison.com/course/diploma-in-project-management) – Comprehensive, no-cost diploma covering full project lifecycles with real-world applications.
- [OpenLearn Project Management Modules (The Open University)](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/project-management) – Free, flexible units on key topics like risk and stakeholder management.
- [SSGI Project Management Certification](https://www.6sigmacertificationonline.com/project-management-certification/) – Practical, self-paced alternative to PMP with case studies and lifetime access.

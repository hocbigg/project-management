## Introduction

Project management is the discipline of turning strategic intent into reality under conditions of uncertainty and finite resources. While ongoing operations sustain an organization, projects drive change — delivering new products, infrastructure, software, and organizational transformations through temporary, goal-oriented endeavors. Mastering this field requires a balance between technical mechanics (defining scope, scheduling network paths, estimating costs, and tracking earned value) and organizational realities (aligning stakeholders, managing risk, negotiating trade-offs, and leading cross-functional teams).

This curriculum provides a foundational, undergraduate-level education in project management for independent learners. It assumes no formal business background or prior management training. The material is designed around practical comprehension and applied workflows rather than academic research methodology or institutional memorization.

## Navigating the Curriculum

The curriculum is structured around the socio-technical lifecycle of a project, moving progressively from concept to evaluation:

- **Foundations and Initiation:** Begin with foundational lifecycle models and governance structures, followed by project initiation and scope definition. These sections teach you how to evaluate business cases, draft project charters, identify key stakeholders, and define clear boundaries to prevent scope creep.
- **Detailed Planning and Controls:** Next, progress through baseline planning, resource leveling, and risk, quality, and procurement management. This phase covers quantitative and analytical methods, including Work Breakdown Structures (WBS), Critical Path Method (CPM) network scheduling, bottom-up cost estimation, and contingency planning.
- **Execution and Closeout:** Move into execution, monitoring, and control, where you will learn to track project performance using Earned Value Management (EVM), administer integrated change control, and facilitate iterative workflows using Agile and Kanban frameworks. Conclude the operational cycle with project closure, focusing on contract closeout, asset handover, and post-project retrospectives.
- **The Human Dimension:** While listed as a distinct subject, leadership, stakeholder communication, and principled negotiation underpin every phase of the project lifecycle. You can study this section concurrently with the technical subjects or treat it as an integrative capstone on interpersonal and political dynamics.

Each subject offers a mix of learning resources, pairing comprehensive open-access textbooks and canonical reference volumes with free-to-audit online courses and university courseware. You do not need to consume every listed resource sequentially; choose the format (text-driven or video-guided) that best fits your learning style, using alternative texts to clarify complex topics such as network diagramming or earned value mathematics.

## Curriculum

### Foundations of Project Management

Introduces core concepts of projects, project lifecycle models, organizational governance structures, and the distinct characteristics of predictive, agile, and hybrid delivery methods.

[Project Management: The Managerial Process (Erik W. Larson & Clifford F. Gray)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260238860) - The canonical undergraduate textbook providing a balanced socio-technical view of project selection, organizational strategy, and delivery life cycles.

[Project Management (Adrienne Watt / BCcampus)](https://opentextbc.ca/projectmanagement/) - A comprehensive, peer-reviewed open textbook covering fundamental concepts and lifecycle models, serving as a free alternative to Larson & Gray.

[Foundations of Project Management (Google / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/project-management-foundations) - A practical, entry-level online course covering the project manager role, project phases, and operational frameworks, freely accessible in audit mode.

[Introduction to Project Management (AdelaideX / edX)](https://www.edx.org/course/introduction-to-project-management) - An alternative university-level introductory MOOC covering the fundamentals of project lifecycles and delivery contexts, freely accessible in audit mode.

### Project Initiation and Scope Definition

Covers strategic project selection, business case development, stakeholder identification and analysis, requirements elicitation, and establishing formal project boundaries through the project charter.

[Project Management: The Managerial Process (Erik W. Larson & Clifford F. Gray)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260238860) - Core reading on strategic alignment, project screening criteria, charter formulation, and scope checklists to prevent project drift.

[Project Initiation: Starting a Successful Project (Google / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/project-initiation-google) - An applied course walking through the creation of measurable project charters, stakeholder analysis matrices, RACI charts, and scope boundaries, freely accessible in audit mode.

[Project Management (Adrienne Watt / BCcampus)](https://opentextbc.ca/projectmanagement/) - A free open textbook providing practical guidance and templates for stakeholder mapping, business requirements definition, and initiation approvals.

### Project Planning, Scheduling, and Baselines

Explores Work Breakdown Structures, activity sequencing, Critical Path Method network analysis, schedule baselines, cost estimation techniques, and resource leveling.

[Project Management: The Managerial Process (Erik W. Larson & Clifford F. Gray)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260238860) - Definitive text detailing top-down and bottom-up cost estimation, CPM and PERT network scheduling, and resource-constrained leveling.

[Project Planning: Putting It All Together (Google / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/project-planning-google) - An applied video-based course focusing on schedule creation, milestone mapping, budgeting, and planning artifact documentation, freely accessible in audit mode.

[Project Management (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/1-040-project-management-spring-2009/) - Undergraduate course materials providing lecture notes, quantitative problems, and case studies on network diagrams, scheduling mathematics, and cost baselining.

[Project Management (Adrienne Watt / BCcampus)](https://opentextbc.ca/projectmanagement/) - Open-access chapters detailing Work Breakdown Structure hierarchy, critical path calculation, and budget allocation methods.

### Project Risk, Quality, and Procurement

Examines qualitative and quantitative risk analysis, risk response strategies, quality planning and control standards, and procurement contract types alongside vendor management.

[Project Management: The Managerial Process (Erik W. Larson & Clifford F. Gray)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260238860) - Core text covering risk identification matrices, contingency reserve allocation, and outsourcing management.

[Project Management: A Managerial Approach (Jack R. Meredith, Samuel J. Mantel Jr. & Scott M. Shafer)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781119803836) - A comprehensive alternative textbook providing deeper coverage of quality control systems, contract structures, and vendor negotiation.

[Project Management (Adrienne Watt / BCcampus)](https://opentextbc.ca/projectmanagement/) - A free open-access guide detailing practical risk response strategies, quality metrics, and procurement planning workflows.

### Project Execution, Monitoring, and Control

Focuses on tracking project performance using Earned Value Management, administering integrated change control, reporting status via dashboards, and running iterative iterations via Agile and Kanban frameworks.

[Project Management: The Managerial Process (Erik W. Larson & Clifford F. Gray)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260238860) - Canonical guide to Earned Value Management (EVM), schedule and cost variance calculations, performance indexing, and formal change control.

[Project Execution: Running the Project (Google / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/project-execution-google) - Practical training on day-to-day progress tracking, managing scope changes, tracking issues, and building project status dashboards, freely accessible in audit mode.

[Agile Project Management (Google / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/agile-project-management) - Builds directly on traditional control methods by teaching adaptive execution, Scrum roles and ceremonies, backlog refinement, and Kanban flow management, freely accessible in audit mode.

### Project Closure and Organizational Learning

Details administrative closeout, deliverable handover, formal customer acceptance, post-implementation retrospectives, lessons learned documentation, and long-term benefits realization.

[Project Management: The Managerial Process (Erik W. Larson & Clifford F. Gray)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260238860) - Essential reading on final deliverable acceptance, post-project audit methodology, team performance evaluations, and knowledge transfer.

[Project Management: A Managerial Approach (Jack R. Meredith, Samuel J. Mantel Jr. & Scott M. Shafer)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781119803836) - An alternative text offering detailed frameworks for project termination decisions, contractual closeout, and organizational learning.

[Project Management (Adrienne Watt / BCcampus)](https://opentextbc.ca/projectmanagement/) - A concise open-access overview of administrative closeout procedures, transition planning, and project post-mortem templates.

### Project Leadership, Communication, and Team Dynamics

Develops interpersonal leadership capabilities, team motivation and conflict resolution models, stakeholder communication planning, principled negotiation, and professional ethics.

[Project Management: The Basics for Success (UC Irvine / Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/project-management-basics) - A practical course combining situational team leadership models, communication channels, and team motivation mechanics, freely accessible in audit mode.

[Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)](https://archive.org/details/gettingtoyesnego0000fish_z1w8) - The foundational, freely readable open-library text on principled, interest-based negotiation and conflict management with project stakeholders.

[Project Management: The Managerial Process (Erik W. Larson & Clifford F. Gray)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260238860) - Core textbook chapters addressing social capital, organizational politics, team development stages, and ethical responsibilities in project leadership.
